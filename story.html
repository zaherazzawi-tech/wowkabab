Our story.

Some food you can fake. Shawarma isn't one of them.

It has to be stacked by hand in the morning, seasoned right, and carved off the spit while the outside is still crackling. That's the slow way, and it's the only way we do it. Walk into our kitchen in Masi Plaza and the first thing you'll see is the spits turning — beef on one, chicken on the other, all day.

Stacked by hand. Carved to order.

The rest of the menu follows the same rule. Kabobs go over a real grill. Falafel gets fried when you order it, not before. The garlic sauce is made here. The bread is warm when it hits your hands.

We keep it casual because good food doesn't need a show. Come in a suit, come in gym clothes, bring the kids — there's a menu for them too, and enough vegan options that nobody at the table settles.

That's the whole story. Fresh fire, sharp knife, and food worth saying "wow" about.
